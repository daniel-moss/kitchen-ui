# Radio

We use **Radio** inside
<a href="/?path=/docs/components-radio-radioitem--docs" target="_top">RadioItem</a>.
It can also be used inside radio-like local components in designs.

## Anatomy

Radio is a circle frame with another circle inside on selected states. The
frame is `--size-4` (16px) with `--border-radius-full` corners and a 1px
`--gray-a7` border when unselected. Selected fills the frame with `--gray-12`
and shows a `--size-1_5` (6px) `--gray-1` center dot.

<Canvas of={RadioStories.Anatomy} />

## Behaviour

Every value has 7 states: default, focused (keyboard only), hovered, pressed,
error, disabled and loading. A **selected** circle keeps one look on hover and
press — only the focus ring is added. **Error** is a `--tomato-9` border when
unselected and a `--tomato-9` fill when selected. **Disabled** dims the circle
to 30% and shows the not-allowed cursor. **Loading** is a plain `--gray-a3`
fill with no dot and the busy cursor.
