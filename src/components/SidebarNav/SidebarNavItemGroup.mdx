## Overview

Clicking or tapping the group header expands and collapses the group. The
header is a `stackHeader`
<a href="/?path=/docs/components-sidebarnav-sidebarnavitem--docs" target="_top">SidebarNavItem</a>
(built from `icon` + `label`), the children are `stackItem` items. The header
and the items stack `--size-0_25` (1px) apart. The examples are live — click
the headers.

<Canvas of={SidebarNavItemGroupStories.Collapsed} />

While the group is expanded, the header carries the active emphasis (solid
icon, strong label, caret turned down) — without a fill at rest.

<Canvas of={SidebarNavItemGroupStories.Expanded} />

`headerActive` is separate from the open state: it gives a closed header the
same emphasis when its stack holds the current page.

<Canvas of={SidebarNavItemGroupStories.WithActiveItem} />
