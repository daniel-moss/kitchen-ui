# Chip

We use **Chip** as a small interactive element for single or multiple
selections. It works as a toggle — clicking or tapping switches it between
inactive and active.

<Canvas of={ChipStories.Interactive} />

```tsx
<Chip active={selected} onClick={() => setSelected((v) => !v)}>
  Chip
</Chip>
```

## Anatomy

The bare minimum is text only. Chip has two sizes: **md** (`--size-7` (28px) —
caption 13/20, `--size-2` (8px) padding, `--size-1_5` (6px) gap) and **lg**
(`--size-8` (32px) — body 14/20, `--size-2_5` (10px) padding, `--size-2` (8px)
gap). Both have `--border-radius-1_5` (6px) corners.

<Canvas of={ChipStories.Sizes} />

Chip supports a **left slot** — an
<a href="/?path=/story/components-icon--default" target="_top">Icon</a>
or an avatar. The icon is solid, size 14, and inherits the label color; all its
parameters are editable. The avatar size is fixed at **xxs (16px)**, but any
avatar is supported.

## Behaviour

### Active and inactive

A chip is inactive or active, and clicking or tapping switches between them.
Inactive is a `--surface-interactive-default` fill with a 1px `--gray-a2`
border and the `--box-shadow-xs-down` shadow; active is a `--gray-a2` fill with
a 1px `--gray-12` border and no shadow. `active` also sets `aria-pressed`, so
pass it on chips that work as toggles.

<Canvas of={ChipStories.ActiveInactive} />

### States

Each value has 5 states: default, focused (keyboard only), hovered, pressed and
disabled. **Inactive** hover/press fill `--surface-interactive-hover` /
`--surface-interactive-press` and drop the border and shadow. **Active**
hover/press step to `--gray-a3` / `--gray-a4` and keep the border. **Focus** is
the Button-style ring — a 2px `--gray-a12` ring with a 2px gap and an inner fill
block. **Disabled** dims to 30% with the not-allowed cursor.

### Loading

`isLoading` swaps the label for a skeleton bar and the left slot (if one is
set) for a `--gray-a3` circle — 14px for an icon, 16px for an avatar. The chip
is not interactive and shows the busy cursor.
