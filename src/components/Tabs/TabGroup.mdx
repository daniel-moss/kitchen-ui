# TabGroup

TabGroup navigates between sections of content — the pages of a job, the saved
views of a list, the parts of a side panel. It holds a row of Tabs, one of them
selected, and the content under it changes with the choice. Unlike
<a href="/?path=/docs/components-segmentedcontrol--docs" target="_top">SegmentedControl</a>
it is not a control: each tab leads somewhere different, rather than showing the
same thing another way.

## Anatomy

A row of tabs with no container of its own — no fill, no border, no padding. The
group places them (`--size-0_5`, 2px apart as pills; `--size-6`, 24px apart
underlined), marks which one is selected and owns the keyboard; everything else
belongs to the surface it sits on.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Anatomy} />

## Styles

Two styles, and they are not interchangeable.

**pill** marks the selected tab with a soft `--gray-a3` shape on
`--border-radius-2` (8px) corners — the same radius as SegmentedControl's track.
It carries its own edges, so it can sit anywhere: beside a title, inside a
panel, next to buttons in a toolbar. Use it for switching that is not attached
to the content below it.

**underlined** marks the selected tab with a 2px `--gray-12` line along the
bottom of the row. The line belongs to the edge it sits on, so this style is for
the row that **labels the content directly underneath it** — the views above a
table, the sections above a page body. It needs a bar with a bottom edge to land
on: the tab has no height of its own and fills the row it is given.

One rule follows from that: when two rows of tabs stack, they must not use the
same style. Two identical rows leave no way to tell which level you are reading.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Styles} />

## Full width

By default each tab hugs its label and the row hugs its content.

`isFullWidth` stretches the row to its container and the tabs share the width
equally. Use it inside a narrow surface — a panel, a drawer — where a hugging
row would sit oddly in the space.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.FullWidth} />

## Sizes

Two sizes: **md** (`--size-8`, 32px) and **lg** (`--size-9`, 36px). The step up
changes the side padding — `--size-2_5` (10px) becomes `--size-3` (12px) — and
nothing else; both carry `body-500-compact` (14/20).

**Underlined has one size.** With no height of its own and no horizontal
padding, the two sizes would draw the same tab, so the style ignores `size`.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Sizes} />

## Content

Three switches build a tab: `children` for the label, `slotLeft` for an icon or
an avatar before it, `counter` for a number after it. That makes five shapes —
label, slot and label, label and counter, slot and label and counter, and slot
alone.

A counter needs a label. A number beside an icon with no word reads as a
statistic, not as a place to go — the component says so in the console.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Content} />

## Left slot

Takes an
<a href="/?path=/story/components-icon--default" target="_top">Icon</a>
or an avatar before the label, `--size-2` (8px) away from it. All the Icon's
parameters are the caller's — **a selected tab no longer changes the icon's
weight** — and the avatar is **xs** (20px).

An icon with no colour of its own follows the label through every state. An icon
the caller coloured keeps that colour.

Slot only drops the label and keeps the same box. It needs an `aria-label`, or
the tab has nothing to announce.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.LeftSlot} />

## Selection

The group owns the value. `value` with `onChange` drives it from outside,
`defaultValue` leaves it to the group, and the selected TabItem gets `selected`
injected — a tab does not decide on its own.

Selection lifts the label from `--text-subtle` to `--text-strong` and adds the
style's own mark: the `--gray-a3` shape on a pill, the line on an underline tab.
A pill's shape is drawn inside its own bounds; an underline tab's line sits on
the row's bottom edge, on top of the divider that separates the bar from the
content — and the group **slides one shared line** between tabs (200ms), so only
the line moves.

The row is a tablist: `role="tablist"` on the group, `role="tab"` with
`aria-selected` on each tab, one tab stop — the selected tab — and the arrow
keys move the focus and select as they go. Home and End jump to the ends.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Selection} />

## Warning

`warning` points at something behind the tab that needs attention — a detail
that is missing, a value that does not add up. The icon and the label take
`--text-warning`, and it holds in every state, so an inactive tab still shows
the problem.

The slot draws the solid `warning` icon and replaces `slotLeft`. The state is
not a colour applied to any icon — it is that icon.

It needs a label. On a slot-only tab a coloured glyph alone says nothing, and
the component says so in the console.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Warning} />

## Loading

`loading` replaces the label with a `SkeletonTypography` bar and the slot, if
there is one, with a circle — 14px for an Icon, the avatar's own 20px otherwise.
The tab is not interactive and shows the busy cursor.

A tab loads because its content is not known yet — a label that comes from data,
a count that has not arrived. It loads unselected: a tab you cannot read is not
one to land on.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Loading} />

## States

Engaging a tab lifts its label from `--text-subtle` to `--text-strong`. From
there the two styles differ, on purpose:

| pill | rest | hover | press | focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| inactive | — | `--gray-a2` | `--gray-a3` | `--gray-a2` |
| selected | `--gray-a3` | `--gray-a4` | `--gray-a4` | `--gray-a4` |

An **underline** tab has no fill in any state — the line and the label carry it
all, so nothing competes with the line.

Disabled dims the whole tab to 30% and it stops responding.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.States} />

Focus draws the ring: on a pill, a 2px `--gray-12` stroke at its bounds, a 2px
gap, then its own fill redrawn inside with `--border-radius-1` (4px) corners; on
an underline tab, the stroke at its bounds and nothing else, since there is no
fill to redraw. Neither changes size, so a focused tab stays in line with its
neighbours.

<Canvas of={TabGroupStories.Focus} />

State never changes the tab's size.

## Usage

A TabGroup belongs to the surface whose content it switches.

| Where it appears | What it switches |
| --- | --- |
| TopBarNav | the phase of a list — open and closed, active and inactive |
| TopBarView | the saved views above a list |
| SidePanelNavigation | the sections of the object a panel shows |
