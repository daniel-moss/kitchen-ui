# StepItem

We use **StepItem** within
<a href="/?path=/docs/components-stepitemgroup-stepitemgroup--docs" target="_top">StepItemGroup</a>.
It represents one step in a multi-step form.

## Anatomy

A StepItem is a **status icon**, a **label**, and a 2px **status line** across
the top. The icon is a solid 14px glyph; the label is caption 13/20. It has a
`104px` min-width and fills the available width.

<Canvas of={StepItemStories.Anatomy} />

## Behaviour

### Responsiveness

A step fills the width of its container until it reaches its `104px` min-width.
Inside a StepItemGroup, the steps stretch equally and their status lines form
one continuous progress bar.

<Canvas of={StepItemStories.Responsiveness} />

### Statuses

Five statuses drive the icon, colors, and status line:

- **incomplete** — `--gray-a10` icon, `--text-subtle` label, no line
- **current** — `--gray-12`
- **completed** — `--jade-9`
- **warning** — `--amber-9`
- **error** — `--tomato-9`

<Canvas of={StepItemStories.Statuses} />

### Text

The label truncates with an ellipsis when it does not fit; hovering shows the
full text in a tooltip.

<Canvas of={StepItemStories.Text} />

### States

A step is interactive when given an `onClick` — it renders a `<button>` with
hover, focus, and press states (in a stepper the completed and error steps get
one). Hover is `--gray-a3`, focus is `--gray-a3` with a 2px `--gray-12` ring,
press is `--gray-a4`, and disabled dims to 30%.

<Canvas of={StepItemStories.States} />

### Loading

The loading state looks the same across every status — a skeleton label and a
loading icon.
