# StepItemGroup

We use **StepItemGroup** to provide navigation within multi-step forms. It lays
a row of
<a href="/?path=/docs/components-stepitemgroup-stepitem--docs" target="_top">StepItem</a>s
out as a stepper.

## Anatomy

The band has left/right padding and top + bottom hairline strokes; there is no
gap between steps. Inside is a responsive **inner container** — centered, capped
at `528px` — that holds the step stack. The steps stretch equally and their
status lines join into one continuous progress bar.

<Canvas of={StepItemGroupStories.Anatomy} />

## Behavior

### Responsiveness

The inner container is responsive and centered, capped at `528px`. The steps
stretch to fill it; once they hit their `104px` min-width, they extend beyond
the container and it scrolls horizontally.

<Canvas of={StepItemGroupStories.Quantities} />

### Navigation

When the steps overflow, the stack scrolls and keeps the **current** step in
view — it is auto-centered as the flow advances.

<Canvas of={StepItemGroupStories.Overflow} />

### Interactivity

By default the flow is one-directional: the user cannot skip ahead, but can jump
**back** to a completed step. Flexible navigation (jump to any step) is also
possible.

**StepItemGroup does not enforce this itself** — the consumer decides which
steps get an `onClick`. In the example below the completed steps are clickable
and the current and later steps are not.
