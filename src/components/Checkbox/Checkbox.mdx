## Anatomy

Checkbox is a square with a **check** icon on selected states and a **hyphen**
icon on intermediate states. The box is `--size-4` (16px) with
`--border-radius-1` (4px) corners; the glyphs are solid 10px icons in
`--gray-1` on a `--gray-12` fill.

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.Values} />

## Behavior

Every value has 7 states: default, focused (keyboard only), hovered, pressed,
error, disabled and loading. A **filled** box (checked or indeterminate) keeps
one look on hover and press — only the focus ring is added. **Error** is a
`--tomato-9` border when unchecked and a `--tomato-9` fill when filled.
**Disabled** dims the box to 30% and shows the not-allowed cursor.
**Loading** is a plain `--gray-a3` fill with no icon and the busy cursor.
